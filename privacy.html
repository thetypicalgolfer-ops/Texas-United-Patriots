Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Texas United Patriots PAC ("Texas United Patriots," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or interact with our organization, how we use that information, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

Information you provide. We collect information you choose to give us, such as when you sign up for updates, volunteer, contact us, or make a contribution. This may include your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, employer and occupation (where required for contributions), and any message you send us.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. We may collect this information using cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To communicate with you about our movement, events, and campaigns;
  • To process and acknowledge contributions and volunteer sign-ups;
  • To respond to your questions, comments, and requests;
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website;
  • To comply with applicable campaign-finance and other legal requirements.

Disclosure of Contributions

As a political action committee, we are required by federal and/or Texas state campaign-finance law to collect and publicly report certain information about contributors, which may include your name, mailing address, employer, occupation, and the amount and date of your contribution. Information reported to the Federal Election Commission or the Texas Ethics Commission becomes part of the public record.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and communications, with other organizations as permitted by law, and when required to comply with a legal obligation, enforce our policies, or protect the rights, property, or safety of our organization or others.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve your experience. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the site.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may opt out of our email communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in any message we send. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal recordkeeping obligations that apply to us.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the website after any change indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our contact page or email us at hello@texasunitedpatriots.org.
